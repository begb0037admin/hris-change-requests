Change Request — 38-Day Annual Leave Balance Migration: Chemistry (Pilot) and GLAM (Priority List)

1.	Details - What is changing?

== Migrating department workgroups onto a 38-day annual leave balance in PeopleXD/WFM, department by department, in a controlled rollout rather than allowing departments to make balance changes independently.

Phase 1 - Chemistry (pilot): 137 workgroups. Work Group updates on 1-2 October, leave-year rollover on 5-7 October, Chemistry staff-record updates from 8 October.
Phase 2 - GLAM: priority list of 39 workgroups - 28 Bodleian and 11 Ashmolean. Scheduled separately, after the Chemistry sequence and rollover complete, subject to confirmed capacity. Two Ashmolean rows require a holiday-scheme check during processing (change scheme only if required; rename Work Groups as requested regardless).

2.	Justification - Why is the change necessary?

== Chemistry and GLAM have requested migration to a 38-day annual leave balance. To keep this controlled and auditable, departments must not make balance changes independently - this CR is the mechanism for HR Systems to apply the change. Chemistry is being run first as the pilot to prove the approach before GLAM's rollout.

6.	Impact - Effect of not applying the change?

== Chemistry and GLAM staff remain on their current (incorrect/unrequested) annual leave balance rather than the agreed 38-day entitlement, and departments may attempt to make the change independently outside of the controlled process.

- Leave-year rollover (5-7 October) must complete and be reconciled before Chemistry staff-record updates or any GLAM scheduling decision.
- Two Ashmolean rows need a holiday-scheme check during GLAM processing - contained data check, not a reason to reopen the GLAM priority list.
- GLAM timing depends on post-rollover capacity - sequencing risk, not a technical risk.
